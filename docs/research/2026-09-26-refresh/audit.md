Scope: 40+ load-bearing claims re-checked against primary sources (DeepSeek docs/changelog/ToS/issue tracker, Apple doc JSON + archive guides, libdispatch header, RFC 9110/6202, Prometheus/Google/AWS/WidgetKit docs, and the actual repo files cited as precedent). Verdicts are `SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNSUPPORTED / STALE`.

## Claims that should NOT be trusted without further checking

1. **Any assertion that DeepSeek has, or could have, a push/webhook/conditional-GET/usage channel reachable with an API key.** The negative is documented-absence plus one un-reproducible probe. The probe (raw headers on `GET /user/balance`, including an `If-None-Match` round-trip) is the single most valuable missing artifact and is shared by briefs 1 and 3.
2. **The `platform.deepseek.com/api/v0/usage/*` endpoints and the `200 + {"code":40003}` rejection.** Third-party reports (one CodexBar contributor's issue + two 0–1★ repos), no vendor statement, untestable without a session token; the brief's "DeepSeek's own org states" framing overstates authorship.
3. **"Balance has a seconds-to-minutes ingestion delay."** Single unsourced third-party doc that also says the opposite ("near real-time"); it is the sole basis for the "sub-minute polling is pointless" interval argument.
4. **Currency of the dynamic-limiter FAQ sentence.** Verbatim, but from a 2024-10-10 capture; the live FAQ is unreadable programmatically. Treat as historical, re-check before using it to justify a rate policy.
5. **The platform low-balance e-mail/SMS alert.** CSDN blogs only; no official page. Do not put it in user-facing copy.
6. **"A status-item `LSUIElement` app will be App-Napped, so its timer is unreliable."** Plausible and well-hedged but not Apple-documented; decide with the Activity Monitor App Nap column + `timerfires`, not with this inference.
7. **`Timer`-stall ⇒ "menu bar timers stop while the menu is open."** The doc text is verified; the specific failure mode is inference from field reports, not Apple.
8. **`schedulingAppliesToLowPowerMode` non-existence at SPI level, macOS-12 availability of `isLowPowerModeEnabled`, `ProcessInfo.thermalState` on macOS, any absolute energy number, post-wake timer promptness, `NWPathMonitor` under App Nap, `Task.sleep` tolerance→leeway plumbing.** All correctly marked UNKNOWN/open — keep them that way.
9. **Precedent rows labelled "search summary only"** (and CursorMeter's README-derived details) plus the negative "no tool implements wake handling."
10. **The three briefs' recommended intervals as if they were one finding.** They differ (60 s foreground / 5–15 min idle vs 30/60 min backstop + 90 s window vs 10–30 min + 5 min if snappier) and are judgment, not sourced. Pick one policy explicitly; do not average them.

## Overall evidence-quality verdict

The evidence quality is high and unusually well-labelled: nearly every load-bearing Apple, RFC, Prometheus, Google, AWS and WidgetKit claim I re-checked is verbatim-accurate, brief 2's trickiest API semantics (one-sided `Timer.tolerance`, two-sided dispatch leeway with the `interval/2` clamp, wall-clock vs uptime sleep behaviour, NBAS's symmetric "before or after" window, the `schedulingAppliesToLowPowerMode` absence) all survived independent inspection, and brief 3's repo-level precedent claims check out line-for-line against the actual source files — including the README-vs-code contradiction, which is exactly the kind of catch this exercise should reward. The real weaknesses cluster in brief 1 and at the seams between briefs: the DeepSeek verdict rests on **absence of documentation plus a probe neither brief could reproduce**, its two "PARTIAL" nuances and its freshness premise are third-party-only (one of them circular with CodexBar via issue #654), one key policy input is a two-year-old FAQ snapshot, and the three briefs hand the decision-maker three different interval numbers framed as conclusions rather than as one reconciled policy. Nothing I found contradicts a recommendation, but several claims currently sound more settled than the underlying evidence is — chiefly "no push/conditional GET," "ingestion delay," and "the app will be App-Napped." Treat the answer to the product question ("poll, don't subscribe; refresh on look with a deferrable backstop") as **supported**; treat the specific numbers and the two DeepSeek side-channels as **provisional**.
